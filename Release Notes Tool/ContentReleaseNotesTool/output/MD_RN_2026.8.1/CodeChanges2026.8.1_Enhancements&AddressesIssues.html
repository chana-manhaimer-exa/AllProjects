Announcements

  • As part of the planned September release, several product category updates will be introduced. Refer to the attached Product_Category_Update_Announcement.pdf document for the complete list of category reclassifications.
  • We are deprecating 46 Exabeam CIM Audit activity-types by Q3 and consolidating them into a single unified Activity-Type: audit-log:success/fail for all audit events. The impacted parser is exabeam-audit-json-alert-case-success. For the full list and migration details, please refer - Exabeam_Activity_Type_Deprecation.pdf.
  • Field Extraction Expressions: We will be introducing built-in enricher expressions for extracting file, process, URL, and operating system sub-fields (e.g., file_dir, file_name, file_ext, process_dir, process_name, os, version, web_domain, top_domain, sub_domain, uri_path, uri_query, uri), reducing the need for parser-level regex extraction and simplifying parser development.
